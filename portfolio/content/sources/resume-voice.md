title: Project — Voice-Native Legal RAG Assistant (2026)
---

Voice-native RAG proof-of-concept for legal research: real-time audio pipeline on LiveKit + Pipecat with Google ADK multi-agent orchestration, Deepgram STT / ElevenLabs TTS for low-latency conversational turns. File-type-aware chunking (Reducto OCR for scanned/image documents), OpenAI text-embedding-3-large embeddings in Turbopuffer, hybrid BM25 keyword + semantic retrieval with citations surfaced in the Word add-in.
