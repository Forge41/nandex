AutoInterviewer (Python, Django, Temporal, pgvector, Next.js, LiveKit, Terraform, AWS): turns a resume into a tailored interview plan in 5s; scaled to 1,000 concurrent interviews with 300ms live-audio round-trip latency and a 35% interview-to-offer conversion lift for pilot recruiters.
